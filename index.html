Privacy Policy

Simple Strum Book · Effective 18 October 2026

Simple Strum Book is a songbook for guitar players. It works entirely on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, where it stores it, and what it never does.

The short version

  • Your songs, settings and recordings are stored only on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • Data only leaves your device when you choose to export, save or share it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Songs: titles, artists, lyrics, chords, tabs, keys, tempos, collections and favourites, kept in a local database.
  • Audio: recordings you make in the app and audio files you attach to a song.
  • Preferences: settings such as theme, Reader text size, scroll speed, recently used chords and whether you have seen the how-to guide.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it, access it or recover it.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so you can record song ideas and practice takes. The microphone is used only while you are recording, after you start a recording yourself. Recordings are saved on your device and are never uploaded. You can deny or revoke microphone access in your device settings at any time; everything else in the app keeps working.

Files you choose

When you attach an audio file, the app reads only the file you pick in the system file picker and copies it into the app's storage. The app does not browse or read any other files on your device.

Exporting and sharing

When you export a song as a PDF, save a recording to your phone or share a recording, the app hands the file to your device's own save or share screen. Where it goes next is your choice, and the app you send it to handles it under its own privacy policy.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information, usage data, device identifiers or location. It contains no third-party advertising or analytics services.

Backups

Your device's operating system may include app data in its own backups (for example Android or iCloud backup), depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by your device's provider, not by us.

Deleting your data

You can delete any song or recording inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
